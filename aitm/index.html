Research software

Artificial Intelligence in Translational Medicine

AITM is an interactive R Shiny platform for biomarker-based predictive modelling and translational medicine research.

It provides a reproducible, user-friendly workflow for exploring biomarkers, developing clinical signatures and evaluating their diagnostic and prognostic performance—without requiring direct programming.

Core capabilities

From biomarkers to clinical interpretation

01

Biomarker selection

Machine-learning workflows identify informative biomarkers and support the construction of predictive or prognostic signatures.

02

Clinical performance

ROC analysis, data-driven cut-off estimation and diagnostic-accuracy measures help evaluate model performance.

03

Risk stratification

Patient-level signature scores support clinically interpretable risk groups and interactive model-coefficient exploration.

04

Survival analysis

Kaplan–Meier curves and Cox proportional-hazards models assess progression-free and overall survival.

AITM runs online through Shinyapps.io.
