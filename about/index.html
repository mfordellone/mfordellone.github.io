About me

Mario Fordellone is a biostatistician and clinical data scientist at the University of Campania “Luigi Vanvitelli”.

His work combines statistical methodology with clinical and epidemiological research, supporting multidisciplinary teams from study design and data analysis to the interpretation and publication of results.

He holds a PhD from Sapienza University of Rome. His research focuses on survival analysis, causal inference, oncology, high-dimensional biomedical data, dimensionality reduction and interpretable machine-learning methods, with a strong commitment to translating quantitative evidence into clinically meaningful knowledge.

Current focusBiostatistics · Clinical research · Healthcare analytics · Precision and translational medicine
