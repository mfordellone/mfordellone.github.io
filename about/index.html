About me

Mario Fordellone is a biostatistician and clinical data scientist at the University of Campania “Luigi Vanvitelli”, working at the intersection of statistical methodology, clinical research and epidemiology.

He supports multidisciplinary research teams throughout the full study lifecycle—from research questions, study design and data management to statistical modelling, interpretation and scientific publication. His work is driven by a practical goal: turning complex biomedical data into robust and clinically meaningful evidence.

He holds a PhD in Methodological Statistics from Sapienza University of Rome and a second-level Master’s degree in Statistical Methods for Clinical Research and Epidemiology from the University of Campania “Luigi Vanvitelli”, both supported by research on innovative methods for classification and complex data analysis.

His research interests include survival analysis, causal inference, oncology, high-dimensional biomedical data, dimensionality reduction, fuzzy clustering and interpretable machine learning. Across academic appointments in Naples, Rome and Padua, he has developed experience in methodological research as well as in applied collaborations spanning precision oncology, chronic diseases, nutritional epidemiology and public health.

Alongside research, he teaches medical statistics and experimental research methods in medicine and allied health programmes, with particular attention to clear interpretation, reproducible analysis and the responsible use of quantitative evidence.
